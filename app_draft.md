Optimal Freight Application 
Developed at Tennessee State University

Aiming for tennessee state 


Suppose i am a truck driver i want to go from A position to B position with the safest, without having crashes (quickest without delay or congestions). 

If the path has accident it shows hotspots, also the path should be economics with gas and other reasons, also its recommending if it has rest areas. 
It should show the dynamic path when driver moves and shows path which will be shortest(trave time).


Input:
1. We will have a hotspot(historcial) map input from the gis plotting, for crash too we will get from gis
2. Economical analysis from excel sheet
3. Rest areas and the gases stations could be found from the google maps and other reliable sources
4. With all the sources, our map should be dynamics and should reroute after moves with standard algorithm used by google maps and the apple maps or other maps.
